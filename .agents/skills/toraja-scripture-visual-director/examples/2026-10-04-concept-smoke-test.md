## Reading synthesis
The weekly material binds a tended vineyard that fails to yield the owner's hoped-for fruit, accountability to the owner, longing for restoration, and Paul's Christ-centered revaluation of status. The intended fruit is **justice, truthfulness, compassion, humility and integrity**, not literal grapes alone. The visual should support faithful conduct as an answer to God's care, not imply that agricultural abundance guarantees spiritual merit.

## Three candidate scenes
### 1. Kebun yang Dipercayakan
One continuous ancient vineyard with carefully worked soil, healthy vines and a distant watchtower. A human caretaker attends the vine responsibly, without the scene claiming a literal historic depiction. Strong single focus on stewardship. Title-negative-space in quiet upper-left sky within central 4:3; right and left outpaint add distant hills and vineyard rows. Risk: could read as mere agriculture; mitigate with concept note and image focus on caretaking rather than glamour.

### 2. Buah yang Diharapkan
Close-to-medium painterly shot of one mature grapevine bearing beautiful ripe clusters, supported by visible crafted vineyard details. The richness of the fruit symbolizes *desired character* rather than prosperity; one coherent place and one moment, **not** two contrasting bunches or split panels. The title lives in calm soft upper-left background in 4:3; 16:9 only extends leaves/trellis/landscape. Risk: pretty grapes without ethical meaning; accompany with visual rationale and prefer concept 1 when purpose-specific fidelity is prioritized.

### 3. Penggarap yang Bertanggung Jawab
A modest single caretaker kneels to tend a living vine within one vineyard. The image embodies humble ongoing responsibility; cinematic light, tactile soil, work-worn hands, brush-textured clothes. Title in upper right open sky of 4:3; expansion completes vineyard path and atmospheric horizon. Risk: can overemphasize human effort; balance cues of vineyard already abundantly cared for.

Prefer **1 — Kebun yang Dipercayakan**: it joins God's stewardship motif to the congregation's responsibility while staying legible as one scene, with space for the title and easy horizontal outpaint.
