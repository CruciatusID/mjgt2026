## 02 — 16:9 outpainting prompt
**Input:** approved, final 4:3 artwork.
**Geometry:** center original in same-height 16:9; extend canvas left and right (each 12.5% final width). Retain master pixels wherever feasible.
**Allowed expansion:** [continuations of physical surfaces only]
**Locked:** original subject and silhouette, facial identities, light direction, brush texture, perspective, original title-safe empty area.
**Forbidden:** camera reframe, redraw, new essential objects, captions, wings with competing subjects, seams, texture mismatches.
**Crop-back check:** center 75% of final width must reproduce master and preserve original visual meaning.
