# Evaluation rubric and release gates

## Concept scoring (100 points)
- Faithfulness to supplied theme, readings & objectives: 35
- One clear coherent visual thesis (single scene; not a multi-panel comparison): 20
- Emotional/visual communication and screen-distance focal clarity: 15
- 4:3 integrity + negative space + 16:9 extension practicality: 15
- Correct Toraja/Biblical cultural and historical setting: 10
- Practical paintable painterly 3D direction: 5

Do not let score hide hard failures. **Hard fail** any: contradiction to supplied passage or aim; split-panel/comparison scene; essential object in expandable wing; title drawn into main image; gross cultural/historical confusion; malformed/anatomically disturbing human subjects; inappropriate religious imagery. Three concepts should be worth considering, with one recommended; revise weak ones instead of submitting three near-duplicates.

## Visual deliverable gates
1. **Main 4:3 independently complete**: Focus subject, emotional weight, and purpose-built safe title area work flawlessly at normal and large projector scales.
2. **Long-distance church screen readability (Focal & Text Clarity)**:
   - *Focal subject silhouette*: Primary subject gesture and essential symbols must maintain crisp, unmistakable silhouettes legible from the rearmost pew (simulating a 10×–20× screen-width viewing distance). No muddy, low-contrast clutter.
   - *Typography readability*: Text size, stroke weight, word spacing, and luminance contrast against the background must ensure effortless reading by the congregation in both well-lit sanctuaries and darkened auditoriums.
3. **16:9 canvas expansion integrity & honest reporting**:
   - Master 4:3 area centrally locked (middle 75% width).
   - Verify whether canvas expansion genuinely pixel-locked the master or performed re-synthesis/re-generation. **Hard reject false claims of pixel preservation if resampling occurred**.
   - If regenerated, run strict differential visual QA: zero lost details, no distorted anatomy, no shifting horizons, no competing wing subjects.
4. **Cinematic Painterly 3D finish**:
   - Palpable, expressive brushwork obvious across all surfaces (skin, garments, stonework, foliage, atmosphere).
   - Convincing 3D volume, sculptural form, and natural anatomy; strictly zero plastic CGI, zero flat 2D painting, zero excessive photorealism.
5. **Contextual & theological fidelity**: People, hands, architecture, props, and botany adhere to the locked brief; scripture and liturgical intent are not misrepresented.
6. **Separate Indonesian title asset**: Exact spelling, flexible layout matched to negative space, color derived from the rendered master, cleanly isolatable (transparent PNG prioritized, solid black/white verified), and tested over both 4:3 and 16:9 crops.

Report three states accurately: **PASS / REVISE / NOT TESTED**. Prompts alone = NOT TESTED for generated-image tests. Never certify visual rendering you have not inspected.
